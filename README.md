# Barrel & Brew – Gap Analysis, Process Mapping & RACI

Business analysis project diagnosing operational breakdowns at Barrel & Brew, a family-owned craft winery, and proposing a structured improvement plan.

## Business Context

Barrel & Brew is a decade-old, family-owned craft winery known for small-batch wines. Over the past year demand has surged — its products are now stocked in over 500 local restaurants — but internal operations have not scaled with that growth, creating production, quality, and morale problems.

## Approach

1. **Gap Identification** – mapped current vs. desired state across seven operational areas (production, quality, employee morale, processes, forecasting, infrastructure, communication).
2. **Root Cause Analysis** – used a fishbone (Ishikawa) diagram to categorise the underlying causes of recurring issues.
3. **Process Mapping** – flowcharted the existing production process to pinpoint bottlenecks.
4. **RACI Matrix** – defined Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, and Informed roles across the team to clarify ownership of each proposed action.
5. **Solution Design** – used a swim lane diagram to map proposed solutions against the teams responsible for delivering them.

## Key Findings

| Area | Current State (Gap) | Desired State |
|---|---|---|
| Production | Frequent delays, missed targets, inventory shortages | Reliable, consistent output |
| Quality | Inconsistent taste, occasional spoiled batches | High, consistent quality |
| Employee Morale | Overworked staff, high overtime, resignations | Empowered, well-supported staff |
| Processes | Manual notebooks & outdated spreadsheets | Streamlined, digitalised operations |
| Forecasting | Informal, intuition-based demand prediction | Data-driven forecasting & planning |
| Infrastructure | Aging tanks, bottling line jams | Modern, reliable equipment |
| Communication | No coordination between sales & production | Aligned cross-team communication |

## Proposed Solution: Three-Phase Plan

**Phase 1 – Fix the Operations**
Replace manual tracking with a digital production system; upgrade the bottling line and fermentation tanks to remove reliability risk.

**Phase 2 – Get Smart with Data**
Move from intuition-based to data-driven demand forecasting; integrate production, sales, and inventory systems; introduce end-to-end quality tracking.

**Phase 3 – Invest in the Team**
Standardise procedures, roll out training on new systems, and formalise cross-team communication channels.
